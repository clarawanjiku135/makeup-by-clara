Beginner Full Face Makeup Guide

Beginner Full Face Makeup Guide

Step-by-step techniques based on Rosina Sharon's Masterclass

1 Eyebrow Shaping & Fill

  • Dot Method: Use a sharp eyebrow pencil (e.g., Davis No. 3). Place 3 guide dots aligned with the nose corner, pupil edge (arch), and outer lash line.
  • Soap Technique: Rub a damp spoolie on bath soap to form a foam, then brush brows upwards in the front and sideways toward the arch to lock hair in place.
  • Filling: Fill in gaps starting from the tail moving inward using soft upward strokes.
  • Clean Up: Clean lower brow with a flat brush and concealer 2 shades lighter than skin; clean inner brow with foundation that matches your exact skin tone.

2 Eyeshadow & Winged Liner

  • Prep: Apply eyeshadow base/concealer from lower lid moving upward and blend thoroughly.
  • 3-Dot Placement: Place 3 dots of matte brown transition shade (outer corner, lid midpoint, inner lid) and connect them gently.
  • Blending: Use a lighter brown matte shade in circular motions along the edge to soften lines.
  • Winged Liner Trick: Apply gel/cream liner along the lash line, then drag gently upward with your fingertips from the lower lash angle for a soft winged effect.

3 Skin Prep & Foundation

  • Prep: Apply a moisturizing SPF 50 sunscreen all over, followed by a pore-minimizing primer on your T-zone.
  • Foundation: Use a damp beauty blender with a small amount of matte foundation (e.g., Maybelline Lumi Matte).
  • Application: Tap gently onto cheeks and work outwards. Blend remaining product down to the neck and hairline using minimal force.
Pro Tip: Never apply heavy foundation directly under the eyes to prevent creasing.

4 Concealing, Highlighting & Setting

  • Concealer: Apply concealer 2 shades lighter under the eyes (inner and outer corners), T-zone, and chin.
  • Blend & Lift: Blend inner to outer corners using upward strokes to create a lifted face look.
  • Set Immediately: Press a matching compact finishing powder (e.g., Maybelline Fit Me Powder) into the under-eye area immediately after blending to lock it in place.

5 Powder Contour, Lips & Setting Spray

  • Contour: Use a powder contour 2 shades darker. Apply from the hairline toward the pupil line using upward sweeping motions.
  • Lips: Outline with a warm brown lip liner, feather it into the corners, apply a pink/nude gloss in the center, and blend with your finger.
  • Lock In: Mist setting spray generously from a distance to fuse all products together for a seamless finish.
